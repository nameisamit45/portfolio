Ferroelectric Memristor-CMOS Crossbar Engine for Sobel Edge Detection

A transistor-level study of programmable, low-voltage, in-memory edge filtering. The architecture uses dual 3×3 crossbars, conductance-state enhancement, and a 7 nm differential readout to increase observable analog contrast without expanding the array.
